3. How to draw [[Plane|Planes]]
	1. Numbers in the brackets are called [[Miller Indices]]
	2. All numbers are reciprocated
		1. $$f(n) = \begin{cases} 0, \infty \\ x, \frac{1}{x} \end{cases}$$
	3. Donated with ()
	4. All Non-zero indices give us a [[Triangular Geometry]] for the plane
	5. 2 Non-zero indices gives us a [[Rectangular Geometry]] for the plane 
	6. Join all the points using lines after plotting them
4. How to find the [[Miller Indices]] of a Plane
	1. Triangular plane must have 3 integers
		1. We must pick an origin that is touching a corner of the triangle but is not touching the plane
		2. All axis must be touching the triangle from that side when picking the origin
	2. Rectangular plane must have 2 integers
		1. Origin must be touching the plane at 2 points minimum
	3. Square plane must have 1 integer
	4. [[Reciprocal|Reciprocate]] all Non-zero numbers
