# Lecture
1. [[Metals]]:
	1. Dense
	2. Good [[Conductor]]s
	3. [[Strong]] and [[Ductile]]
	4. Can be shaped
	5. e.g.: [[Aluminum]], [[Iron]], [[Copper]]
	6. Applications: [[Construction]], [[Automobiles]], [[Electrical Wiring]], [[Aerospace]], etc.
2. [[Ceramics]]:
	1. Non-metialic [[Solids]]
	2. [[Metal Oxides]], [[Metal Carbides]]
	3. [[Hard]] and [[Brittle]]
	4. High [[Melting Point]]
	5. Excellent thermal and Electrical [[Insulator]]s.
	6. e.g.: [[Alumina]], [[Silicon Carbide]], [[Zirconia]], [[Tungsten Carbide]], etc.
	7. Applications: [[Cutting Tools]], [[Aerospace]], [[Biomedical Implants]]
3. [[Polymers]]:
	1. Long chains of organic molecules made of repeating chains of [[Monomers]]
	2. [[Lightweight]] and [[Flexible]]
	3. Low [[Strength]] compared to [[Metals]]
	4. Good [[Corrosion]] resistance
	5. e.g.: [[Polyethylene]], [[Nylon]], [[PVC]], etc.
	6. Applications: [[Packaging]], [[Textiles]], etc.
4. [[Composites]]:
	1. Made from combining two or more distinct materials
	2. Tailored properties
	3. Often stronger and lighter than individual components
	4. Can be designed for specific use
	5. e.g.: [[Carbon Fiber-Reinforced Polymer]], [[Fiberglass]], etc.
	6. Applications: [[Aircraft]], [[Sports Equipment]], [[Automobiles]], [[Wind Turbine Blades]], etc.
5. Modern Materials
	1. [[Graphene]]:
		1. Made from [[Graphite]]
		2. High strength
		3. Flexible
		4. Strong
		5. [[Carbon Nanotube]] and [[Fullerene]]
	2. [[Piezoelectric]] Materials
		1. Respond to [[Mechanical Stress]] with [[Electric Potential]]
		2. Many uses 
			1. e.g.: [[Quartz]]
	3. [[Shape Memory Alloys]]
		1. They remember their original shape
		2. [[Robotics]], [[Aerospace]], [[Biomedical]]
		3. [[Phase Transformation]] (Low and High temperature)
		4. [[Martensite]] structure is remembered
		5. [[Austenite]] is the deformed structure
	4. [[Hydroxyapatite Bioceramic]]
		1. Human Hard Tissue
		2. [[Bioactive]], [[Nontoxic]]
6. [[Integrated Circuits]]
	1. Fixed number of components to create a circuit
7. [[Printed Circuit Boards]]
