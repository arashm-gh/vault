1. Formation of OH is troposphere
	1. $O_3 + \lambda(290-340nm) \to O + O_2$
	2. $O + H_2O \to 2OH$
2. Formation of O_3 in the troposphere
	1. $NO_2 + hv(\lambda \leq 420nm) \to NO + O$
	2. $O + O_2 + M \to O_3 + M^*$
3. Ozone concentration in the Troposphere and Stratosphere
	1. $O_2 + hv(\leq 242nm) \to 2O$
	2. $O_2 + O + M \to O_3 + M^*$
	3. Higher concentration of [[Ozone]] in the stratosphere
	4. Called the [[Ozone Layer]] at peak concentration
4. Ozone formation and destruction
	1. $Photon (hv) + O_2 \to O + O$
	2. $O_2 + O + M \to O_3 + M^*$
	3. $O_2_!$
	4. $O_3 + O \to 2O_2$
5. Stratospheric sink for chlorofluoromethanes: chlorine atom catalyzed destruction of ozone - 1974
6. Catalytic Destruction of Ozone Layer
	1. Certain radicals (Cl, NO) catalytic-ally destroy ozone (O_3) in the stratosphere
	2. Reactions
		1. Chlorine Cycle (From CFCs) $$CF_2Cl_2 + hv \to CF_2Cl + Cl$$
		2. Cl atom works as a catalyst
			1. $Cl + O_3 \to ClO + O_2$
			2. $ClO + O \to Cl + O_2$
			3. Sum: $O_3 + O \to 2O_2$
		3. One Cl radical can destroy up to 100,000 ozone molecules
	3. CFCs were banned globally by 1996 and replaced by HFCs
	4. HFCs will be phased out in the next 30 years
