1. A natural gas plant emits $10,000 MT$ of $CO_2$, $500 MT$ $CH_4$, and $100 MT$ $N_2O$ each year. Using 100-years GWP, calculate their $CO_2-eq$
2. Natural Sources of Greenhouse Gases
	1. Volcanoes: Release [[Carbon Dioxide]]
	2. Oceans: Release Water Vapor
	3. Decomposition: Releases [[Methane]]
3. Anthropogenic Emissions
	1. Burning Fossil Fuels: [[Coal]], [[Oil]], [[Natural Gas]], Transport, Electricity, etc.
	2. Deforestation: Reduces $CO_2$ absorption by trees
	3. Agriculture: Livestock produces methane, and fertilizer use releases [[Nitrous Oxide]]
	4. Industrial Processes: Release various greenhouse gases

5. Acid Rain
	1. $SO_2 + H_2O \to H_2SO_4$
	2. $NO_x + H_2O \to HNO_3$
	3. Damages plants, soil, and aquatic ecosystems
	4. Corrodes buildings and monuments
	5. Worsens respiratory issues
6. [[Photochemcial Smog]] (LA Smog)
	1. Sunlight driven reactions between pollutants
	2. $NO_x + VOCs + Sunlight \to O_3 + PANs$
	3. Volatile Organic Compounds - Light Hydrocarbons that have a very high boiling point but low volatility
	4. Creates a Red Haze
	5. Effects
		1. Respiratory Problems and Eye irritation
		2. Damages Crops and Vegetation
		3. Reduces Visibility
7. [[Urban Smog]] (London Smog)
	1. $HNO_3, SO_x, Oxone, PANs, (NH_3)SO_4, (NH_3)NO_3$
	2. $SO_2, NO_2, VOCs, PM, CO$
	3. Presence of Sunlight is not essential
	4. Vehicular Emissions + Waste Burning + Industrial Emissions
8. $NO_2$ and $O_3$ absorb strongly solar radiation in the troposphere
9. [[Ozone Depletion]]
	1. Formation of $OH$ in the troposphere
		1. $O_3 + \lambda(290-340nm) \to O + O_2$ (Photon hits Ozone in the stratosphere/troposphere)
		2. $O+ H_2O \to 2OH$
	2. Formation of $O_3$ in the troposphere
		1. $NO_2 + hv(\lambda \leq 420nm) \to NO+O$
		2. $O+O_2 + M \to O_3 + M^*$ (M absorbs excess heat)
	3. Stratosphere (Next lecture)
