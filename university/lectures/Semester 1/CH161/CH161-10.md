1. Tools to improve health and safety

			2. Decide who might be harmed and in what circumstances $\to$

			4. Record the significant findings $\to$

		4. How to do a risk assessment
			1. Decide who might be harmed
			2. Evaluate the risks and adequacy of existing controls ([[OSHA]], [[EPA]], [[FCA]], [[CPSE]], etc.)
			3. Record findings
			4. Review the assessment

			1. Very Likely

			1. Very Rare - <1/year

			3. Unusual - 1/month
			4. Occasional - 1/week
			5. Frequent - 1/day
			6. Continuous - constant

2. Hazard
	1. A source - working on a height
	2. A substance - handling of chemicals
	3. A part of a machine - circular saw blades
	4. A method of work - lifting method
	5. A form of energy, or a situation - overburden on the edge of excavation
3. Harm 
	1. Includes death, injury, physical or mental ill health, damage to property or the environment, loss of production, or any combination of these.
4. Risk
	1. A measure of the likelihood that the harm from a particular hazard will occur, considering the possible severity of the harm,
	2. Risk is expressed as:
		1. Risk = Likelihood of Occurrence \times Harm
5. Consequences
	1. Fatal - Death
	2. Major - Irreversible
	3. Minor - Reversible
	4. Negligible - First Aid 
6. Permit to Work
