#Lecture 
1. Particulate Reinforced
	1. Difference is the size of the particle
	2. 1 Micron -> 1000 Nanometers
	3. Large particle ($>0.1 \mew m$) and dispersion strengthened (0.01 to $0.1 \mew m$)
	4. Carbide + Steel = Very Strong
	5. Rules of Mixtures:
		1. Estimate properties of composite by volume fraction of matrix and reinforcements
	6. Elastic modulus falls between upper and lower limits
		1. $E(u) = E_mV_m+E_pV_p$ -> Force applied in the transverse axis
		2. $E(l) = \frac{E_mE_p}{V_mE_p + V_pE_m}$ -> Force applied in the longitudinal axis
		3. **Is strain increasing or stress decreasing?**
2. Fiber Reinforced
	1. $l_c = \frac{\sigma^*_t d}{2 \tau_c}$
		1. $\sigma^*_t d$ = Ultimate force the fiber can sustain
		2. $l_c$ = Critical Length
	2. Mechanical Properties of composites depend on
		1. Mechanical Properties of the fiber
		2. How much load the matrix can transmit to the fiber
	3. Critical Fiber Length
		1. Minimum fiber length at which there is a maximum possible load transfer from the matrix to the fiber
		2. Necessary for effective strengthening and stiffening of the composite
		3. $E_c = E_m(1-V_t) + E_tV_t$
3. General form of rule of mixtures $$X_c= X_mV_m + X_pV_p$$
